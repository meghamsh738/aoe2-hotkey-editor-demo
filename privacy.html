Privacy

AoE2 Hotkey Editor is designed to work locally in your browser. This page explains what the app does and does not collect.

Hotkey profiles stay on your device

When you open an HKP profile or folder, the files are read by the browser for local analysis and editing. The app does not upload your hotkey files, profiles, or exported bundles to the project.

Local browser storage

The app may save drafts, recent imports, practice progress, custom trainers, validation notes, and sound preferences in your browser’s local storage. You can remove this data through your browser’s site-data controls. Clearing site data can remove saved drafts and progress.

Cookies, analytics, and third parties

The editor does not set tracking cookies and does not include third-party analytics. Because there are no non-essential cookies to consent to, the editor does not show a cookie banner. Links to GitHub and source references open third-party sites whose own policies apply after you leave this app.

Hosting and server logs

The public website is hosted by the GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata, such as an IP address and browser information, in its infrastructure logs. The editor itself does not send profile contents to the host.

Questions or requests

For a bug report, accessibility concern, or privacy question, use the project’s GitHub feedback forms.
